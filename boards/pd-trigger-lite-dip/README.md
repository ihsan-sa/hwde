# pd-trigger-lite-dip

A 25 x 21 mm, 2-layer USB-C PD trigger. Plug in a PD charger and it passes VBUS straight to two
2.54 mm output holes at 5, 9, 12, 15 or 20 V (3 A max). The voltage is picked with a 5-position
DIP switch: exactly one switch ON, set before plugging in. It is pd-trigger-lite with the solder
links replaced by the switch. Controller: WCH CH224A. Full owner guide, with the switch table:
`fab/pd-trigger-lite-dip-guide.pdf`.

| Switch ON (others OFF) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Output | 5 V | 9 V | 12 V | 15 V | 20 V |
