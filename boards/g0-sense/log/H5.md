## Questions for the owner

1. **Order 5 pieces with economy PCBA at roughly USD 43?** The recommendation is
   yes, but this is the owner's money and the owner's call.
2. **Accept the one durable waiver** (`check_thermal` on U1)? It is the only
   error-severity finding on the board. Evidence in `reports/verify-waivers.json`
   and summarised in `fab/README.md` section 7: the checker's model credits only
   same-net top copper and ignores the 810.9 mm2 B.Cu spreader under the
   regulator; against the vendor tables for this package the rise is 40.8-42.8 C
   inside the declared 45 C budget, Tj 82.8 C rated and 109.7 C at the
   entitlement-abuse case against a 125 C limit. It is also unsatisfiable by any
   geometry on this outline (needs 446.4 mm2 credited; the whole board can fund
   439.9 mm2). The P8 fresh-context reviewer independently ratified it.
3. **Accept the 4 residual silk misattributions** (R1, R2, C10, D1)? Fixing them
   needs a placement change on a fully routed DRC-perfect board. `fab/README.md`
   section 5 carries the full J3/J4 pinout as the mitigation.
