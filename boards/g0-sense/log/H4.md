## What was fixed rather than waived

1. **check_pdn "VBUS has no decoupling capacitors"** was a DATA gap, not a board
   defect: C1 (100 nF at the connector - the only capacitance the USB-C 10 uF
   attach limit permits ahead of the PTC) was missing from decoupling.json's
   inventory. Added the real association after measuring 5.52 mm to J1's VBUS pad,
   inside check_decoupling's 7.5 mm threshold; check_decoupling still passes.
2. **Two VBUS insufficient_transition_vias** fixed in copper: 2 vias -> 3 at each
   transition, with measured clearances (0.865/0.874 mm, hole-edge gap 0.600 mm).
3. **VBUS pour_neckdown ("necks to 0.10 mm")** was a per-zone checker artifact.
   Verified independently by eroding the union of the VBUS fills plus J1's pad
   copper by 0.4 mm - the full 0.8 mm IPC-2152 test at the conservative 1.5 A
   fault basis: each pad still reaches all three of its vias. Real minimum in that
   copper is 0.680 mm and it gates a 0.33 mm2 dead lobe with no via and no pad.
   constraints.json was therefore NOT relaxed - the board meets 1.5 A physically.

## The one waiver

**check_thermal thermal_area on U1** (57.8 C modelled rise vs 45 C allowed).
Waived as model pessimism, ratified by the independent reviewer:
- The model credits only same-net TOP copper. It returns 144 C/W where AMS1117
  p5 Table 1 measured 80, and 123 C/W where TI LM1117 Table 9-2 measured 84.
- This board has an uncredited **810.9 mm2 B.Cu GND spreader** under U1, and the
  knowledge record states the spreading layer need not be electrically connected.
- At the conservative vendor anchor (84 C/W): rise 42.8 C, inside the 45 C budget.
  Tj = 82.8 C at the governing 0.51 W case, 109.7 C at the entitlement-abuse
  0.83 W case, 61.8 C realistic - against a 125 C limit.
- Also proven unreachable: the model needs 446.4 mm2 credited copper; assigning
  EVERY free front mm2 on the whole board yields 439.9 mm2.
- Not merely waived: the pour was still grown 171 -> 250 mm2 (65.3 -> 57.8 C).
- Rejected deliberately: a B.Cu +3V3 island and thermal vias would help, but they
  carve the ground return plane of a 2-layer board, trading a PASSING
  check_return_path for margin the measured data says is not needed.
