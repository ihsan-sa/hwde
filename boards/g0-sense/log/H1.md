## Riskiest decision

Taking the brief's literal ">= 300 mA rated" as the LDO thermal design point
(0.51 W) rather than the ~150 mA realistic load. It buys the SOT-223/tantalum
combination and sizes the board's densest region. Justification: the Qwiic port
is an unfenced 3V3 output, so the rated case is reachable in the field, and
AMS1117 holds Tj 71-86 C at 40 C ambient where SOT-23-5 alternatives hit 134 C
with no copper fix available.

## Two decisions research FORCED, both against the earlier plan

1. **I2C pull-ups 2.2k -> 1.5k.** The record backing 2.2k was refuted against its
   own bus budget: UM10204 Eq 1 at tr=300 ns gives Rp(max)=354/Cb[pF] kOhm, so the
   ceiling is 1.77k at 200 pF and 2.2k needs Cb <= 161 pF. 1.5k holds to 236 pF and
   clears both floors. A second reader recomputed 1.5k independently.
2. **BOOT0 no-strap -> a populated 10k pull-down on PA14/pin 19.** The "no strap
   needed" reasoning rested on a record whose only source was a community.st.com
   forum page; a fresh reader refuted it, and RM0454/AN2606 cannot be acquired
   (st.com times out from this container, verified three ways). Rather than design
   on unverified knowledge, the board is made correct under EITHER option-byte
   state. Cost: one 0402, 330 uA on SWCLK.
