## The three warnings and what was done (full text: `reports/erc-waivers.md`)

1. **W1 - no ESD array on the J2 Qwiic SDA/SCL. WAIVED.** Product scope requires
   met-or-waived and no waiver existed. Waived on: the Qwiic/STEMMA-QT ecosystem
   norm is a bare JST SH with no array; both silicon endpoints are 2 kV HBM with
   internal clamps; the recorded environment is indoor bench, not a field-exposed
   port; and adding an array now costs a new Extended MPN + setup fee, a library
   pull and a re-opened green schematic against a warning-severity,
   field-reliability-only exposure. Retrofit stays cheap later (`add-part`), and
   the 1.5k pull-up budget has room for an array's few pF.
2. **W2 - carried "SHT4x VDD slew <= 20 V/ms" verify item. CLOSED, accepted risk,
   no hardware change.** Three findings. (a) The reviewer's "provenance not on
   file" is wrong: it is in `research/records/sht4x-vdd-slew-power-up.yaml`,
   sourced to `research/sources/HT_DS_Datasheet_SHT4x.pdf` p9 Table 4, read
   visually twice and marked verified - the reviewer grepped the P3 extraction
   JSON, a different artifact. (b) The ~40-68 V/ms worst case is an unreachable
   bound: I_limit/C assumes the LDO drives its output cap independently of its
   input, but the AMS1117 is a series-pass follower, so at cold start the +3V3
   edge is the USB source's VBUS ramp through C2, not the LDO's current limit.
   (c) The datasheet's own failure mode is "MAY lead to a reset" - and a reset
   during the power-up ramp is the state a power-up is meant to produce. The
   limit's real bite is in-operation excursions and hot-plugging a DOWNSTREAM
   breakout onto a live Qwiic rail, which is that breakout's decoupling problem.
   Hardware options were priced and rejected (a series R at U3 VDD is killed by
   the 75 mA on-chip heater; more bulk on C3 buys nothing against (b)). Belt and
   braces goes in documentation: "issue an SHT4x soft reset (0x94) at init".
3. **W3 - J3/J4 DNP intent was not KiCad-native. FIXED, not waived.** DNP lived
   only in a custom `Variant` field, so a native `kicad-cli bom`/POS export would
   have put two THT headers into an SMT-only JLC economy order. Native `(dnp yes)`
   is now set on J3/J4 through a new `schlib.Sheet.mark_dnp()` (in_bom and
   on_board stay yes - the lands must exist and the headers belong in the
   documentation BOM), and `refdes_class: {J3, J4 -> hand_install}` was added to
   the header line in `parts.json` so `bom_cpl.py` keeps them out of the assembler
   upload and the CPL. **Proof of no collateral damage:** netlists exported either
   side of the change compare `identical: true`, 35/35 nets, 0 membership diffs.
