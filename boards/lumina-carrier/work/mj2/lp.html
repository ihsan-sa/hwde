LPJG0926HENL 100/1000 Base-T Single Port RJ45 PoE+ Gigabit MagJack (POE+,Tab Down, Green/Yellow Led)

The LINK-PP LPJG0926HENL is a 10/100/1000BASE-T Gigabit RJ45 Integrated Connector Module (ICM) engineered for IEEE 802.3at PoE+ (Power over Ethernet Plus) applications. By integrating the RJ45 physical interface, Ethernet magnetics, common-mode filtering, and LED indicators into a single package, this module helps reduce BOM component count, shorten high-speed signal paths on the PCB, and improve overall assembly efficiency. Compared with discrete RJ45 connectors and standalone LAN transformers, the integrated design also reduces placement tolerance issues and simplifies automated PCB assembly. It enables simultaneous Gigabit Ethernet data transmission and IEEE 802.3at PoE+ power delivery over a single Ethernet cable.

Unlike conventional Ethernet magnetics designed solely for data transmission, PoE-enabled magnetic modules must simultaneously support high-frequency differential signaling and the continuous DC current imposed by power delivery. The LPJG0926HENL utilizes a dedicated 8-core transformer architecture (1CT:1CT turns ratio) to maintain stable magnetic characteristics under these operating conditions. This architecture helps prevent magnetic core saturation under PoE+ loads while providing balanced signal coupling and impedance matching across all four twisted pairs. Additionally, the integrated magnetics provide the essential electrical isolation required between the Ethernet PHY circuitry and the external cable interface, supporting reliable operation and protecting sensitive silicon. Furthermore, integrated AutoMDIX support automatically detects MDI/MDIX cable configurations, eliminating manual crossover cable selection during installation and simplifying field deployment.

Mechanically, the LPJG0926HENL utilizes a 90-degree right-angle (side-entry) design with a Tab-Down (Latch Down) orientation, optimized for Through-Hole (THT) assembly. Configured without external EMI spring tabs, it allows for a flush-mount fit against custom device enclosures and flat faceplates. Mounted on an FR4 base with a built-in T-Post retaining pin, it improves PCB retention during automated wave soldering and long-term mechanical handling. Integrated Green (Link) and Yellow (Activity) LEDs provide direct visual network diagnostics at the port.

Compliant with IEEE 802.3at, RoHS, REACH, and UL standards, and operating within the 0°C to +70°C commercial temperature range, this ICM is well suited for PoE-enabled edge equipment. Typical applications include industrial Ethernet switches, PoE-powered IP cameras, embedded computing platforms, Industrial PCs (IPC), IoT gateways, and custom Raspberry Pi 3B+ Power-over-Ethernet HATs.
