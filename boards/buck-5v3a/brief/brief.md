# Brief: buck-5v3a

Build a synchronous buck converter board.

- Input 7-18 V, nominal 12 V, via 2-pin 5.08 mm screw terminal.
- Output 5 V at 3 A continuous, to a second 2-pin 5.08 mm screw terminal.
- Include reverse-polarity protection on the input.
- Ambient up to 50 C, no forced airflow.
- Board size <= 50 x 40 mm.
