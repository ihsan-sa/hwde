The AO4407A uses advanced trench technology to provide 
excellent RDS(ON), and ultra-low low gate charge with a 25V 
gate rating. This device is suitable for use as a load switch 
or in PWM applications. Standard Product AO4407A is Pb-free 
(meets ROHS & Sony 259 specifications).
