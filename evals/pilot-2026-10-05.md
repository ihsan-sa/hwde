# e2e cost pilot, 2026-10-05 (usbc_ldo, hwde arm)

The owner approved 5 runs ($200 total, $40 per run). Only run 1 has been done,
because a track iteration is killed after one hour, and one full hwde run takes
longer than that. The other 4 runs are waiting on how a run may outlive an
iteration.

| run | seed | wall | cost | score | how far | why it stopped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20261004T140428Z (#75) | 1 | 37.5 min | $24.12 | 29.96 | schematic | the allowlist refused the board's own generator script (fixed in #75) |
| 20261005T073146Z | 1 (rerun) | 46.2 min | ~$15.7 (est.) | 84.28 | P6 (layout) | stopped by hand at 08:18Z, 12 min before the iteration's timeout |

The rerun's score splits as electrical 1.0, layout 0.21 and cost 1.0. Its board had
placement but no finished routing when it was stopped, so layout is the open
category.

Its cost is an estimate. `claude -p` prints no result when it's killed, so the
driver recorded `cost_usd: null` (exit 143). I copied the nested run's
transcripts out of the live sandbox before stopping it, summed their token
usage by model, and priced it at the per-token rate each model showed in the
#75 run's `modelUsage`, with weights in 1, out 5, cache read 0.1 and 1h cache
write 2. The token counts are measured, but the price weights are my
assumption.

## Cost per run, and the suite

The rerun spent about $0.34 a minute and was at P6 after 46 min. I think a full run
to P9 takes 70-110 min, so it would cost about $25-40, close to the $40 cap.

- 30-run suite (5 briefs x 3 seeds x 2 arms): about $750-1,200
- 60-run suite (5 briefs x 6 seeds x 2 arms, or 3 seeds x 4 arms): about $1,500-2,400

The bare arm hasn't been measured here, and it may be cheaper, because it
spawns no subagents. The suite isn't approved, and nothing here starts it.
