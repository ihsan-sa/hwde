## 2026-10-08 [route_cleanup][route_style] An unconditioned DRU clearance rule overrides the netclass, so judge a snap by both
On a copy of PCB-0020-A the off-angle snap left 23 GND/+3V3 segments "blocked" although DRC was clean: the netclass says 0.2 mm, but the .kicad_dru carries an unconditioned 0.127 mm clearance rule, KiCad's DRC enforces that rule instead of the netclass, and the router had left 0.13-0.16 mm gaps. Every one of those segments already had negative spare under the 0.2 model before any bend. route_cleanup now accepts a route at full netclass clearance, or one that comes no closer than the copper it replaces and still keeps what DRC enforces (SnapEnv.hard). Three more traps from the same run: KiCad joins two items when an anchor of one (track or arc end, via centre, pad centre) lies inside the other's copper, not when copper merely overlaps, so a tee is cut only at an anchor; foreign zone fill must not block, because the refill moves it; and a foreign segment the snap already replaced must stop blocking, so the snap sweeps until nothing frees up. Result on that copy: off-angle 110 -> 3, DRC 0/0 before and after. Review then made the fallback conservative: the fallback takes the largest clearance min over ALL .kicad_dru clearance rules, conditioned or not (0020-A's same-footprint J1 rule is one), because DRC's value then depends on the pair, so a conditioned rule can only make it stricter, never looser; no clearance rule at all keeps it off; it is judged per foreign net, not on the single worst gap; and drills keep min_hole_clearance (0.25 mm default) in both models. On the same copy the J1 rule equals the floor, so off-angle stays near 3.
