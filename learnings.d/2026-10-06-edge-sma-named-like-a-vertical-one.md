## 2026-10-06 [placement][connector][mating][3d-model] Edge SMA named like a vertical one skipped the direction check
PCB-0023-A J101/J102/J501 are BAT Wireless BWSMA-KE-P001 ("PCB-end" edge launch, legs straddling the board), footprint `SMA-SMD_BWSMA-KE-P001`. The sma_edge regex wanted EDGE or KWE, so the name fell through to sma_vertical (`SMA-SMD`) and only the finger-room rule ran; nobody checked which way they faced. The copper was right: pads, silk and the datasheet all put the mouth at the edge. The 3D model was wrong: rotate (0 270 180) lays the barrel into the board, so the render the owner reviewed showed three SMAs facing inward, and he caught it, not a check. A kicad-cli render with rotate (0 270 0) puts them out at the edge.
Fixes: KE added to sma_edge; SMD pads on both F.Cu and B.Cu make a connector horizontal whatever its name says; the model's bounding box, through the footprint's offset/rotate/scale as KiCad's 3D viewer applies them (Rz(-rz).Ry(-ry).Rx(-rx), local y = -3D y on the front, as is on the back; both signs checked against renders), must reach at least 1 mm ahead of the contact pads (mating_model_reversed).
Do not judge a model by its bbox centre against the pads: a box is the same turned round or pushed back, and the centre test flagged PCB-0016-A/B J1 (USB-C, 1.2 mm back) and PCB-0025-A J101 (DC jack, 5.6 mm back, mouth correct in a side render). Only "lies wholly behind the contacts" is a reversal a box can prove.
kicad-cli render on the user-space KiCad needs APPDIR=~/.local/kicad10 (kc.render_env), or it renders a bare board and exits 0.
